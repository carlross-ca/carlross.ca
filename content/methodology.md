## Public Record

- Portfolio TWR
- Benchmark TWR
- Difference
- Drawdown
- Risk breach days
- Return drivers as percent of beginning NAV

## Return Basis

Returns are time-weighted, CAD-based, and compared against the S&P 500 Total Return index translated into CAD where available in the reporting database.

External cash flows are separated from performance so the return record reflects portfolio results.

## Publishing Cadence

Performance records are monthly and automated after reporting checks pass.

Each monthly record includes 1 month, trailing 3 month, trailing 12 month, and since-inception figures. Quarter-end and year-end performance are therefore already embedded in the monthly record.

Manual PM notes are separate and optional.
