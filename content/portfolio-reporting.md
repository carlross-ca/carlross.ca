## Want help understanding your portfolio numbers?

I offer one-on-one help with tracking returns, understanding trading costs, and organizing brokerage records. I can also walk you through the tools and reporting methods I use myself.

We can work through questions like:

- What has my portfolio actually earned after deposits and withdrawals?
- How much am I paying in trading costs?
- How do I combine records across accounts and currencies?
- Can I make my reporting simpler?

My [public performance record](/performance/) shows the kind of reporting I build.

## Working together

Start with a casual conversation. Tell me what you’re trying to understand or improve.

If paid work makes sense, we’ll agree on the scope and price beforehand. That might be a single session or help building a reporting tool.
